Welcome to Indirect Associations! Here's how to play...

This is a puzzle all about inference and analogy. Your goal is to find the analogy between four "hub" words: A is to B, just as C is to D. The only problem is that you don't know any of the words.


What you have access to are words related to the hub words:

For each hub word, you have two opposing words, which relate to the hub in opposite directions. For example, if the hub word is STATE, you may be looking for opposing words COUNTRY and CITY. They are all governmental territories, lying along the size axis: COUNTRY → STATE → CITY. The hub word lies in the middle of the extremes!

You also have access to one adjacent word, which is related to the hub in some way. For example, for the hub word STATE, an adjacent word could be PHASE, as in state/phase of matter.

Arrange the words into hub groups, then arrange those to take the form of the broader analogy!


You have 6 total submissions! Any hub with all three slots filled can be submitted. Confirmed hubs lock into a coloured block you can drag around the board.

(There are some extra "?" fields in the center of each hub and each analogy where you can type! Use this to keep notes and keep track of your ideas. It has no effect on the puzzle!)
