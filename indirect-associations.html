Indirect Associations

You have 12 words and a board shaped like two horizontal lines. Your job is to do two things: group the words into four hidden hub words, then arrange those hubs to form an analogy — A is to B, just as C is to D.

Goal 1 — Find the hub groups

Each hidden hub word comes with three clue words:

Two axis words — they sit on opposite ends of a shared scale, with the hub in the middle. For example, the hub STATE sits between COUNTRY and CITY on a size scale:

COUNTRY  →  STATE  →  CITY
← bigger                 smaller →

One sideways word — related to the hub through a completely different meaning. For STATE, that might be PHASE (as in a phase/state of matter). It won't fit the axis at all — it's a separate connection.

Drag or tap words into the hub slots on the board. Each hub has two axis slots (↑↓) and one sideways slot (adj). The adj word must go in the adj slot — but either axis word can go in either ↑↓ slot.

Goal 2 — Find the analogy

Once hubs are confirmed they become coloured blocks. Arrange them on the board so the two hubs on each line form a matching pair — and both lines must run in the same direction.

[PAPER]  is to  [BOOK]
…just as…
[CELL]  is to  [ANIMAL]
paper constructs a book  ·  a cell constructs an animal

You can submit a pair arrangement even if some hubs aren't confirmed yet — partial credit is given for any correct groupings.

6 submissions total. Each submission checks any completed hub groups and the current line arrangement. Confirmed hubs lock in place and can be tapped or dragged to a new position.

The small ? fields at each hub centre and between the hubs are scratch space for your own notes — they have no effect on the puzzle.
