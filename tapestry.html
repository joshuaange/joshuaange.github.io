Welcome to Tapestry!

The set of human knowledge is not disparate. It builds on itself across fields and domains, often in ways that are imperceptible to most people. One could not dismiss, for example, the age of the earth without first dismissing the archeological record, and therefore radioactive decay, and therefore the entirety of nuclear physics. The sum of human knowledge forms a complex and interlocking web through fundamental principles, history, and application. It forms a tapestry.

Goal : Find the connections

This puzzle aims to emphasize the linking across fields. Every concept on the board is directly connected to others. Note: this is not superficials connections like "country of origin" or "invented by...", but deep fundamental connections between topics. Perhaps they are both fundamentally based on the same mechanism. Perhaps they are examples of the same phenomena. Perhaps one is necessary for the other to be possible.

Hover over a topic (or tap it, on mobile) to see what it means and drag connections from one to the other, forming a web of human knowledge that is 10 connections long.

Propose as many connections at once as you like (or click a connection you've proposed to undo), then hit Submit to check them. There's exactly one way the whole web fits together. Once you've found it all, that's the shape you'll see!
